01 / About me

Curious by nature.
Committed to growth.

Hello, I’m Baraka Tuyisenge. I am a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Africa International University (AIU) in Kenya, passionate about using technology to solve real-world problems and create practical digital solutions.

My interests span web development, software development, networking, cybersecurity, and information systems. I enjoy turning ideas into functional applications while continuously improving my programming, problem-solving, and technical skills.

Through my academic journey and independent learning, I have developed experience with technologies and concepts including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, PHP, databases, data structures, networking, Linux, and cybersecurity fundamentals. I also enjoy building projects that address practical challenges faced by students and communities.

My long-term goal is to become a skilled cybersecurity and technology professional, capable of designing secure, reliable, and user-friendly systems that create meaningful impact.
